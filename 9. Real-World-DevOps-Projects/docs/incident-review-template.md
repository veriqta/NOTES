# Veriqta incident review

## Impact and detection
Affected business actions, declared SLO, first observed failure, and detection signal.

## Timeline
UTC time | observation | action | owner | result

## Failure chain
Trigger, dependency failures, retry/capacity effects, and controls that contained or amplified impact.

## Recovery decisions
Rejected actions, approved runbook, execution identifier, rollback conditions, and independent verification.

## Data correctness
Required markers, missing writes, idempotency/reconciliation results, and measured RTO/RPO assumptions.

## Follow-up work
Action | owner | completion condition | verification method

## Evidence
Sanitized metrics, traces, logs, deployment/plan identifiers and screenshots with private details removed.
